# JAPN 2089

> **Course title:** JAPN 2089 *Twentieth century Japan: History, state and society*
> (HKU, School of Modern Languages and Cultures; Semester 1, 2026-27; coordinator
> Prof. Nakayama Izumi; 6 credits; English-medium elective; 100% coursework).
> Course description: a broad historical survey of Japan and its society from the
> 1850s to the beginning of the lost decades that began in the early 1990s.

## Folders

- `materials/` — the course's own readings as provided; the local course folder is
  described in [`materials/README.md`](materials/README.md).
- `notes/` — my study guides and summaries, including per-reading digests of the
  whole reading set in [`notes/digests/`](notes/digests/).

There is no `revision/` pack for this course: it is assessed by 100% coursework
(reading responses and essays), not by quizzes, so the study material here is
written notes rather than interactive pages.

Two extraction notes worth remembering when quoting from these files:

- `doc.pdf` (Silverberg) has **no text layer at all** — it is a page-image scan.
  Read it as images; page N of the scan is journal page 29+N.
- The JSTOR PDFs (Gordon, and the earlier files) have a **right-margin-clipped text
  layer**. Re-write the PDF through Ghostscript to widen the media box before running
  `pdftotext`, or long lines silently lose their last words.
