## Materials

**No PDFs are copied into `materials/`.** The lecture slides, the textbook chapters and
the tutorial decks are the property of their authors and of The University of Hong Kong,
and the full set is ~41 MB (Textbook 29 MB, Slides 7.9 MB, Tutorial 3.9 MB), which does
not belong in a notes repo. So only this inventory is committed, and the files themselves
stay in the local course folder at
`/home/elijahdelgado/Documents/HKU/LING 2027`, organised as `Textbook/`, `Slides/`,
`Tutorial/`, `Reference/` and `Notes/`. `Reference/` holds the official IPA chart (Kiel
2015), added 25 Oct 2026; `MATERIALS-AUDIT.md` in that folder records exactly what is and
is not on disk as of the same date.

exactly as provided by the course, and list them below.
