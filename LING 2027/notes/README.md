# LING 2027 — Notes

Study guides and summaries for LING 2027.

## Suggested naming

`NN-<topic>.md` to match the topic it covers, e.g. `04-alternations.md` for
Alternations. The leading number is the revision pack's own numbering, not a course
topic number: the course's topic sequence runs Topic 0 Introduction, 1 Phonetics,
2 Abstraction / contrast / predictability, 3 Phonotactics, and alternations is the
topic that follows on from Topic 3. So `04` slots the note in beside the pack's Topic 04
pages, and the note is linked from the course README.

## Index

- [`04-alternations.md`](04-alternations.md) — Alternations (Zsiga Ch. 11.2–3): the
  week-6 class and short quiz 4, plus the midterm material it belongs to.

## Per-reading sets

The section-by-section study material lives in the local course folder,
`LING 2027/Notes/`, as a three-file set per reading: `NN-<section>-Notes.md` (the
teaching content), `-Extension.md` (glossary, comparison tables, method, discussion) and
`-Practice.md` (problems with a full answer key). Current sets:

- `Ch11.1-*` — phonotactic constraints (Zsiga §11.1).
- `Ch11.2-3-*` — analysing alternations and the ten types (Zsiga §11.2–3), adapted from
  `04-alternations.md` and the Topic 04 pages to sit beside the Ch11.1 set.

These are kept out of the repo for the same reason as the PDFs: they are course-derived
working notes, and the repo carries the digest (`04-alternations.md`) and the revision
pack instead.
